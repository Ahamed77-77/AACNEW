OUR STORY

History & Mission

Three decades of unwavering commitment to Islamic education and spiritual growth, founded in the heart of Sri Lanka's North Western Province.

Our Sacred Mission

Islam is a vibrant and divinely blessed religion capable of addressing societal needs through time. We established our institution to ensure Islam thrives as a living religion in Sri Lanka, nurturing scholars equipped to serve the community with wisdom, integrity, and purpose.

1990

Founded

Started with 15 students at Siyambalagasakotuwa Jummah Masjid — the beginning of a lasting legacy.

1991

First Land Donation

40 perches donated by Al-Haj Ahamed Lebbe Mathisam, reflecting deep community trust.

1993

International Recognition

First student scholarship to Al-Azhar University, Egypt — our global reach begins.

1996

First Graduation

4 students received the prestigious 'An-Noori' degree — our first milestone of success.

1999

Major Expansion

Saudi philanthropist Abdullah bin Mansour Zamil expanded the campus to 200 perches.

2000

Modern Campus

Relocated to permanent campus with modern, purpose-built facilities — a new era.

ACADEMIC EXCELLENCE

Comprehensive Programs

A 9-year educational journey integrating Qur'an memorization, advanced Islamic scholarship, and government-accredited modern qualifications.

Al-Qur'an Mmemorization Program

Complete Qur'an memorization over 3 years, covering all 30 Juz' with proper Tajweed. Students memorize 6–12 Juz' per year alongside the school curriculum, earning the honoured Al-Hafiz certification.

3-YEAR PROGRAM

Islamic Studies

Advanced Islamic jurisprudence and theology covering Fiqh, Hadith, Tafsir, and Aqeedah — earning multiple prestigious certifications up to Shari'ah Higher (Moulavi, Al-Alim) level.

6-YEAR ADVANCED

Government Curriculum

Government-accredited G.C.E. O/L and A/L examinations, English language training, IT Diploma, and essential vocational skills — preparing graduates to serve both deen and the modern world.

WORLD-CLASS FACILITIES

Modern Infrastructure

Comprehensive facilities built on 200 perches of land, thoughtfully designed for holistic education, worship, and comfortable residential life.

Our purpose-built campus includes dedicated academic, residential, and spiritual facilities — all maintained to the highest standards to create the perfect environment for learning and worship.

Modern Classrooms

Well-equipped, ventilated classrooms with modern teaching aids. Two-story block at 26×125 ft, designed for both large lectures and intimate study groups.

Prayer Mosque

A beautiful two-story mosque (30×40 ft) serving as the spiritual heart of campus — used for daily prayers, Jumu'ah, and year-round congregational worship.

Student Dormitories

Safe, three-story boarding facility (30×125 ft) providing students a comfortable, secure home — with all essential daily amenities for a productive residential life.

Islamic Library

Extensive collection of Islamic and academic literature in Arabic, English, Tamil, and Sinhala — supporting research, scholarship, and independent study for all students.

Dining Hall

Spacious two-story dining area (30×40 ft) serving fresh, nutritious halal meals daily — prepared under strict hygienic conditions to nourish students' bodies and minds.

Computer Lab

Modern technology centre equipped with up-to-date computers for digital literacy, IT Diploma training, and essential technical skills to prepare students for today's world.
